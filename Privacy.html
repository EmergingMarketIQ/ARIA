    <p>EmergingMarketIQ is operated by Mustansir Yaqub based in Melbourne, Australia.</p>
    <h2>Information We Collect</h2>
    <p>We collect your email address and name when you create an account. We collect usage data including which countries and features you access within the app.</p>
    <h2>How We Use Your Information</h2>
    <p>We use your email address to authenticate your account and send important service updates. We use usage data to improve the app experience. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.</p>
    <h2>AI Generated Content</h2>
    <p>Premium intelligence features are powered by Anthropic's Claude API. Queries you submit to the AI assistant are processed by Anthropic's systems in accordance with their privacy policy at anthropic.com/privacy.</p>
    <h2>Data Storage</h2>
    <p>Your account data is stored securely using Supabase infrastructure hosted on AWS. Data is stored in accordance with applicable Australian privacy laws.</p>
    <h2>In-App Purchases</h2>
    <p>Premium subscriptions are processed securely through Google Play. We do not store your payment information. Subscription management is handled through your Google Play account.</p>
    <h2>Your Rights</h2>
    <p>You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.</p>
    <h2>Data Deletion Request</h2>
    <p>To request deletion of your account and all associated personal data please visit our dedicated data deletion page. We will process all verified requests within 30 days.</p>
