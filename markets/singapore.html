Talent & work permits

Singapore has Asia Pacifics most international talent pool. English is the business language. Government actively manages talent through EP and S Pass frameworks.

  • Foreign workers: Employment Pass for professionals earning SGD 5,600+. S Pass for mid-skilled workers. Quota applies to S Pass holders.
  • Localisation: Fair Consideration Framework requires firms to advertise roles on MyCareersFuture for 14 days before hiring Employment Pass holders.
  • Key consideration: Singapore is using salary thresholds and COMPASS framework to ensure quality of EP applicants while managing local workforce priorities.

How to enter: first four steps

Singapore is the easiest Asian market to enter. ACRA incorporation takes hours. The main decision is choosing the right entity structure.

  1. Incorporate via BizFile+ — ACRA online portal. Private Limited company takes less than 1 day to incorporate
  2. Register with IRAS — Inland Revenue Authority for corporate tax and GST if turnover exceeds SGD 1M
  3. Open corporate bank account — DBS, OCBC or UOB are primary options. Allow 2 to 4 weeks for full setup
  4. Apply for relevant MAS licences — required for financial services, payments and capital markets activities
