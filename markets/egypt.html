Sector priorities

  1. Renewable Energy — Egypt targets 42% renewable energy by 2030. Benban solar park and wind projects creating significant investment pipeline.
  2. Tourism & Hospitality — New administrative capital and Red Sea resort developments creating significant hotel and hospitality investment demand.
  3. Fintech & Digital Finance — Central Bank of Egypt fintech sandbox and 60M+ unbanked population creating significant digital finance opportunity.

Talent & work permits

Egypt has one of Africas largest talent pools with 700,000+ university graduates annually. Arabic is primary business language.

  • Foreign workers: Work permit required from Ministry of Manpower. Quota based on company size. Process takes 4 to 8 weeks.
  • Localisation: Egyptian Labour Law requires minimum 90% Egyptian nationals in company workforce. Exceptions apply for specialist roles.
  • Key consideration: Egypt offers highly cost competitive professional talent, particularly in engineering, IT and finance.

How to enter: first four steps

Egypt offers multiple entry structures. General Authority for Investment is the primary entry point for foreign investors.

  1. Register with GAFI — General Authority for Investment and Free Zones is the one stop shop for foreign investors
  2. Choose your structure — LLC is most common. Free zone options available for export oriented businesses
  3. Register with Egyptian Tax Authority — corporate tax at 22.5% applies. VAT registration required above threshold
  4. Open EGP and USD bank accounts — dual currency accounts essential given FX management requirements
