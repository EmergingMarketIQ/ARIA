Highlights: Semiconductor hub · Bumiputera policy applies · Strong infrastructure

Key industries

Semiconductors & Electronics ↑ Strong growth

Malaysia is the worlds 7th largest exporter of semiconductors. Penang is the Silicon Valley of the East hosting Intel, AMD and Infineon.

Main investors: US, Germany, Japan, Netherlands

Palm Oil & Commodities → Stable

Malaysia is worlds 2nd largest palm oil producer. Downstream oleochemical processing is a growing value add sector.

Main investors: China, India, EU, Japan

Financial Services & Islamic Finance ↑ Growing

Malaysia is the worlds largest Islamic finance market with $500B+ in assets. Bursa Malaysia is SEAs 3rd largest exchange.

Main investors: Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, UK, Singapore

Technology & Digital Economy ↑ Rapid growth

Malaysia Digital Economy Blueprint targeting $22B in digital investment by 2025. Data centre investment is surging.

Main investors: US, China, Singapore, Japan

Sector priorities

  1. Semiconductors & Advanced Manufacturing — NIMP 2030 offering significant incentives for semiconductor and advanced manufacturing investment building on Penang cluster strength.
  2. Data Centres & Digital Infrastructure — Hyperscaler investment in Malaysian data centres surging due to competitive power costs, political stability and connectivity.
  3. Islamic Finance & Fintech — Malaysia is the global centre of Islamic finance creating unique opportunity for Shariah compliant financial products and services.

Talent & work permits

Malaysia has a multilingual professional workforce with strong English proficiency. Talent pool of 15M with good engineering and finance graduates.

  • Foreign workers: Employment Pass for professionals earning MYR 5,000+. Approved by Immigration Department. Process takes 3 to 6 weeks.
  • Localisation: Bumiputera equity participation requirements apply in certain sectors. Employment equity targets apply in government linked companies.
  • Key consideration: Malaysia offers the best combination of English proficiency, technical skills and cost competitiveness in Southeast Asia.

How to enter: first four steps

Malaysia has a straightforward market entry process. SSM registration is fast. Sector specific incentives require MIDA engagement.

  1. Register with SSM — Companies Commission of Malaysia online registration takes 1 to 2 days
  2. Engage MIDA — Malaysian Investment Development Authority for manufacturing and technology investment incentives
  3. Register with LHDN and JKDM — Inland Revenue Board for corporate tax and Royal Customs for SST registration
  4. Apply for expatriate posts — MIDA or relevant ministry approval required before applying for Employment Passes
