Key industries

Tourism ↑ Strong growth

Turkey welcomed 57M visitors in 2023 making it the 4th most visited country globally. Revenue exceeded $54B.

Financial Services → Stable

Istanbul is a regional financial hub. Turkish banking sector is large with $1T+ in assets despite currency challenges.

Main investors: EU, UAE, Qatar, US

Defence & Aerospace ↑ Strong growth

Turkey is a top 10 global defence exporter. Bayraktar drones and indigenous fighter jets reflect growing defence capability.

Main investors: Domestic, Gulf states, Eastern Europe

Sector priorities

  1. Manufacturing & Export — Organised Industrial Zones offer competitive incentives for manufacturing investment targeting EU export markets.
  2. Tourism & Hospitality — Growing visitor numbers and undersupply of quality hospitality infrastructure creating investment opportunity across the country.
  3. Renewable Energy — Turkey targeting 30% renewable energy by 2030. Solar and wind tender pipeline is one of the largest in the region.

Talent & work permits

Turkey has 35M strong workforce with strong engineering and manufacturing skills. Turkish is business language with English in multinationals.

  • Foreign workers: Work permit from Ministry of Labour required. Employer sponsored. Process takes 4 to 8 weeks for approval.
  • Localisation: Minimum 1 Turkish national per 5 foreign employees. Some sectors have specific requirements. Applied by Ministry of Labour.
  • Key consideration: Turkey offers strong manufacturing and engineering talent at competitive European cost levels making it attractive for industrial investment.

How to enter: first four steps

Turkey has a straightforward company registration process. Investment Office of the Presidency provides facilitation for significant investments.

  1. Register with MERSİS — central trade registry system for company incorporation. Process takes 1 to 3 days
  2. Register with tax office — obtain tax number from local tax office before commencing any business activity
  3. Register with SGK — Social Security Institution for employee registration and social security contributions
  4. Consider OIZ location — Organised Industrial Zones offer VAT exemptions, subsidised utilities and streamlined permits
