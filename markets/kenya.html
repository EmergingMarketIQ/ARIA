Highlights: Silicon Savannah tech hub · M-Pesa mobile money leader · Regional HQ destination

Key industries

Technology & Innovation ↑ Rapid growth

Nairobi is Africas Silicon Savannah. iHub, Konza Technopolis and 300+ tech startups make Kenya the continents tech leader.

Main investors: US, UK, China, India

Financial Services & Fintech ↑ Strong growth

M-Pesa transformed mobile money globally. Kenya has Africas highest financial inclusion rate at 83%.

Main investors: UK, US, South Africa, France

Agriculture & Agritech ↑ Growing

Agriculture contributes 33% of GDP. Tea, coffee and horticulture are top exports. Agritech startups are transforming the sector.

Main investors: Netherlands, UK, US, France

Tourism & Conservation ↑ Strong growth

Kenya is Africas premier safari destination generating $2B+ in annual tourism revenue with strong growth trajectory.

Main investors: UK, Germany, US, UAE

Sector priorities

  1. Technology & Fintech — Silicon Savannah ecosystem, young digital population and M-Pesa infrastructure make Kenya the top African tech investment destination.
  2. Renewable Energy — Kenya already generates 90% renewable electricity. Green hydrogen and geothermal energy creating new investment opportunities.
  3. Agriculture & Food Systems — AfCFTA regional trade and food security agenda creating strong demand for agritech and food processing investment.

Talent & work permits

Kenya has East Africas strongest talent pool. English is an official language. Strong university sector producing tech and finance graduates.

  • Foreign workers: Class G work permit required for expatriate employees. Dependent Pass available for family members.
  • Localisation: Work permit quota requires firms to demonstrate no qualified Kenyan is available for the role being filled by an expatriate.
  • Key consideration: Kenya is the preferred East African HQ location for multinationals due to talent availability and English language proficiency.

How to enter: first four steps

Kenya is East Africas most straightforward market to enter. eCitizen portal streamlines registration significantly.

  1. Register via eCitizen portal — company registration takes 1 to 3 days online through the Registrar of Companies
  2. Register with KRA — Kenya Revenue Authority PIN registration for tax purposes is mandatory before operations begin
  3. Obtain sector licences — Capital Markets Authority for finance, Communications Authority for tech, relevant bodies for others
  4. Register with NSSF and NHIF — mandatory social security and health insurance registration for all employees
