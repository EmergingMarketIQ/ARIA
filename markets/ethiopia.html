Key industries

Agriculture & Coffee → Stable

Ethiopia is the birthplace of coffee and Africas largest coffee exporter. Agriculture employs 70% of the population.

Main investors: China, Saudi Arabia, India, Netherlands

Manufacturing & Industrial Parks ↑ Growing

Government industrial parks hosting H&M, PVH and Decathlon garment suppliers offer subsidised utilities and land.

Main investors: China, Turkey, India, EU

Telecoms & Technology ↑ Rapid growth

Safaricom Ethiopia launched 2022. Telebirr mobile money growing rapidly in a market with low financial inclusion.

Main investors: Kenya, UK, US, China

Renewable Energy & Hydropower ↑ Growing

Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam makes Ethiopia Africas largest power producer. Significant power export potential to neighbours.

Main investors: China, EU, World Bank, AfDB

Sector priorities

  1. Manufacturing & Industrial Parks — Government industrial parks offer lowest African manufacturing costs with duty free export access to US and EU markets.
  2. Agriculture & Food Processing — Vast arable land, growing domestic market and export potential making food processing one of Africas best investment opportunities.
  3. Renewable Energy — GERD hydropower surplus and strong solar potential creating power export and green manufacturing investment opportunities.

Talent & work permits

Ethiopia has a young population of 126M with improving education. Amharic is official language. English used in business and government.

  • Foreign workers: Work permit from Ministry of Labour required. Investment permit holders get facilitated work permit processing.
  • Localisation: Ethiopian Investment Law requires employment of Ethiopian nationals except for senior management and technical specialist roles.
  • Key consideration: Ethiopia offers Africas lowest labour costs making it compelling for labour intensive manufacturing despite infrastructure limitations.

How to enter: first four steps

Ethiopia investment entry managed through Ethiopian Investment Commission. Industrial park entry offers the most streamlined route for manufacturers.

  1. Register with EIC — Ethiopian Investment Commission is the one stop shop for foreign investment registration and facilitation
  2. Obtain investment permit — EIC issues investment permit specifying approved business activities and minimum capital
  3. Register with commercial court — business registration with Federal First Instance Court following investment permit
  4. Register with ERCA — Ethiopian Revenue and Customs Authority for tax identification and VAT registration
