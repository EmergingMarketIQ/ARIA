Sector priorities

  1. Garment & Textile Diversification — Bangladesh RMG sector upgrading to higher value technical textiles and knitwear creating investment opportunity in manufacturing technology.
  2. Technology & BPO — Government Hi-Tech Parks and competitive English speaking talent creating growing IT services and BPO investment opportunity.
  3. Pharmaceutical Manufacturing — LDC IP flexibilities until 2033 and low cost manufacturing creating opportunity for generic pharmaceutical production investment.

Talent & work permits

Bangladesh has a young workforce of 70M with improving education levels. Competitive labour costs. English used in business and export sectors.

  • Foreign workers: Work permit from BIDA required. Linked to specific employer. Process takes 6 to 10 weeks.
  • Localisation: Foreign nationals cannot exceed 5% of total workforce in most sectors. BIDA approval required for expatriate hiring above threshold.
  • Key consideration: Bangladesh offers Asias most competitive garment manufacturing labour costs alongside a growing pool of technology talent.

How to enter: first four steps

Bangladesh market entry is managed through BIDA. Special Economic Zones offer streamlined entry for export oriented manufacturing.

  1. Register with BIDA — Bangladesh Investment Development Authority is the one stop service for foreign investors
  2. Consider SEZ location — BEZA economic zones offer duty benefits and streamlined licencing for manufacturing investment
  3. Register with NBR — National Board of Revenue for tax identification and VAT registration
  4. Open bank account with authorised dealer — required for foreign exchange management and remittance approvals
