The Problem

Expanding globally is harder than it should be.

Every cross-border decision today is shaped by fragmented data, outdated reports, and high-cost advisory engagements. For founders, investors, and growth teams, that complexity translates directly into missed opportunities and slow decisions.

01

Information is fragmented

Market intelligence lives across dozens of reports, government portals, and proprietary databases — none of which speak to each other.

02

Emerging markets are hard to read

Regulations, incentives, and on-the-ground realities shift quickly. Western frameworks rarely capture what actually drives growth on the ground.

03

Offshore setup is a maze

Free zones, tax regimes, labour laws, and entity structures vary by country and city. Most founders spend months untangling the basics.

04

Advisory work is slow and expensive

Traditional advisory engagements take weeks and cost five to six figures — long before a single decision is made.

The Solution

One intelligent companion for every cross-border decision.

EmergingMarketIQ brings together economic data, regulatory insight, and on-the-ground market intelligence into a single mobile experience — designed for executives, founders, and investors who think globally and decide quickly.

  • Compare 24 markets side by side Benchmark countries across growth, cost, talent, regulation, and ease of doing business.
  • Identify opportunities first Surface high-potential sectors and corridors before they appear in mainstream reports.
  • Estimate initial setup costs Get indicative cost ranges for setting up offices across each jurisdiction — entity, licensing, real estate, and first-year staffing.
  • Evaluate offshore options Understand entity types, free zones, tax exposure, and operational realities by jurisdiction.
  • Support strategic expansion Move from market scan to a defensible expansion thesis in days, not quarters.

Meet ARIA

Your Advanced Regional Intelligence Assistant — built into the app.

ARIA is the AI engine inside EmergingMarketIQ. It scans the 24 markets every day, distils what actually matters, and gives you executive-ready intelligence on demand — so the work of evaluating a country starts with answers, not searches.

Tax & Treasury Nuances

Ask ARIA about corporate tax rates, withholding, VAT/GST, transfer pricing exposure, and treasury implications for any of the 24 markets — explained the way an executive needs to hear it.

Legal & Regulatory Posture

Form an initial view on the legal and regulatory environment before you start the office setup — entity types, foreign ownership rules, licensing pathways, and sector restrictions.

Compliance & Risk Posture

See a structured view of compliance load and operating risk per market — sanctions exposure, data residency, labour, AML/KYC, and the practical risks teams hit during expansion.

Investment Positioning

Once the fundamentals are clear, use ARIA to refine your investment positioning — sector signals, capital flow patterns, and where the next deployment window is opening.

ARIA refreshes its read on every market every 24 hours. Your view of a country is always today's view — not last quarter's report.
